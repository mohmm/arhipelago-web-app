## 1. What this notice covers

This notice explains where the Smartsense app uses artificial intelligence, what that means for you and what its limits are. It is published by ALTFACTOR SRL, Str. 8 Martie nr. 29, 800258 Galați, Romania, which operates Smartsense, and it applies Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act), which requires that people be told when they are dealing with an artificial intelligence system.

## 2. Where we use artificial intelligence

**Maya, the travel assistant.** Maya answers your questions about places and experiences in Romania. Her answers are generated by a large language model provided by OpenAI.

**Some descriptions of experiences.** A description may be drafted with the help of artificial intelligence and then checked and published by our editors. The app marks these descriptions as generated with AI.

Everything else in the app is prepared by people or calculated without artificial intelligence: the descriptions of places are written by our editors, distances and travel times are calculated by a routing engine from OpenStreetMap map data, and the order of the community posts depends only on their date or on distance.

## 3. You are talking to a machine

Maya is not a person. The app presents her as an artificial intelligence assistant on her screen and in every conversation. Nobody from our team answers in her place, and we do not read your conversations unless you ask us to look at one.

## 4. How Maya finds her answers

Maya does not invent places. Before she recommends or describes something, she searches the places and experiences published in the app and uses their details. Distances and travel times come from the routing engine; when it cannot be reached, Maya gives the straight-line distance and says that it is one.

She is instructed to say plainly when she does not know something and to send you to the official website of a place when there is one. She does not know opening hours, ticket prices, the weather or events, and she has no access to the internet.

## 5. What Maya knows about you

For each answer Maya receives only:

- what you write to her and the latest messages of the same conversation;
- your first name;
- the travel tastes you wrote in Settings, under "What Maya knows about you", if you wrote any;
- the place or experience the conversation is about, when you started it from one.

Maya does not know your email address, your last name, where you are, or what you published in the community. You can change or clear what she knows about you at any time, and delete any conversation. The Privacy Policy, published at [APP_URL]/privacy, explains how long conversations are kept and who receives them.

## 6. How we mark generated text

In the app, Maya's answers appear in her own message bubbles, next to her name and picture, and descriptions drafted with artificial intelligence carry a visible label. In the data the app receives from our servers, every answer from Maya and every such description is also marked in a machine-readable way as generated by artificial intelligence.

## 7. What artificial intelligence does not decide

Artificial intelligence takes no decision about you. It does not moderate the community, it does not suspend accounts and it does not decide what other travellers see. These decisions are taken by people from our team.

## 8. What can go wrong

Language models can make mistakes, even when they sound sure of themselves. Maya may:

- misunderstand a question, especially a short or ambiguous one;
- mix up two places with similar names;
- give a detail that is not in the app, despite her instructions;
- translate a name or a detail imperfectly.

Check anything important before you rely on it. Maya is not suitable for medical, legal or safety matters; in an emergency call 112.

## 9. Human oversight

Our team writes the instructions Maya follows and can change them at any time. The rules that tie her answers to the app's data are built into the app and cannot be switched off from the administration panel. If an answer from Maya seemed wrong, misleading or inappropriate, write to support@smartsense.ro: we will look at it and correct the instructions or the data behind it.

## 10. How Maya is classified

Maya is a general-purpose assistant for leisure travel. She is not used in any of the areas the AI Act treats as high-risk, and she makes no decision about people. What applies to her are the transparency obligations in Article 50, which this notice and the labels in the app fulfil. OpenAI, which provides the underlying model, has its own obligations as provider of a general-purpose AI model.
