## 3. What the app gives you, and its limits

The app is free. There are no paid plans, no purchases inside the app and no advertising.

The descriptions, photos, distances and travel times in the app are prepared carefully, but they are a guide, not a guarantee:

- opening hours, prices, access rules and closures change, often without notice, and the app does not show them. Check them with the place itself, on its official website where the app links to one;
- routes and travel times are calculated from OpenStreetMap map data. They do not know about roadworks, closed paths, weather or traffic, and a walking route is not a statement that the path is safe for you;
- the photos show each place as it was when they were taken.

Mountain, forest and winter outings carry real risks. Plan for the conditions on the day, follow local signs and the instructions of the authorities, and in an emergency call 112.
