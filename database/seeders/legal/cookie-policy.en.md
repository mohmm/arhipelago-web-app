A **cookie** is a small text file a website asks your browser to keep and send back on later visits. **Local storage** is a similar store inside the browser, which the page reads only when it needs to.

Article 4(5) of Law no. 506/2004 allows information to be stored on your device without your consent only when it is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Everything described below falls into that category, or keeps a choice you made yourself. That is why the website shows no cookie banner.

## 2. Cookies on the website

All the cookies below are set by [APP_HOST] itself. We set no analytics or advertising cookies, and no cookies from other companies.

| Name | What it does | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `[SESSION_COOKIE]` | Keeps a member of our team signed in to the administration panel and remembers the website language you chose. Encrypted, and not readable by the page's scripts | 120 minutes after the last visit |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` | Protects the panel's forms against requests forged by other websites. Encrypted | 120 minutes after the last visit |
| `remember_web_…` | Set only when a member of our team ticks "Remember me" when signing in to the panel, to keep them signed in. Encrypted | 400 days, or until they sign out |
| `appearance` | Remembers the light or dark theme, only after you choose one | 365 days |
| `sidebar_state` | Remembers whether the panel's side menu is open or closed | 7 days |

In local storage the website keeps only `appearance`, a copy of the theme you chose, so the page shows it from the first moment.

## 3. Other services the website loads

The website loads its fonts from fonts.bunny.net, a service of BunnyWay d.o.o. in the European Union, and the map images from the OpenStreetMap Foundation. These services see your IP address and browser identification when they send the files, but set no cookies. The Privacy Policy, published at [APP_URL]/privacy, describes them.

## 4. The mobile app

The app does not use cookies. It keeps on your phone, in the app's private storage, only what it needs:

- the sign-in key, so you stay signed in. Signing out deletes it from your phone and from our server;
- the language of the app, and whether you have already seen the welcome screens;
- copies of the photos and map images it has already downloaded, so it does not download them again.

Your position is read only with the permission you give in your phone's settings, and only when you open the "Near me" tab of the community or ask the map to show where you are. You can withdraw the permission at any time from your phone's settings. The Privacy Policy explains what happens to it.

## 5. How to control cookies

You can see and delete cookies in your browser's settings and block them for [APP_HOST]. If you block them, the public pages still work, but the administration panel cannot keep anybody signed in. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept on your phone.
