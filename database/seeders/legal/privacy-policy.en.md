## 4. Emails we send

We write to you only about your account: to confirm your email address, to send you a link to reset your password when you ask for one (it stops working after 60 minutes), and to tell you that your password was changed. We do not send newsletters or advertising. Emails are sent from our own domain, smartsense.ro.

## 5. What other travellers can see

Public, even to people who are not signed in: your first and last name, your profile photo, city and description, everything you publish, the number of your posts, followers and the travellers you follow, and the likes and comments your posts received.

Private, visible only to you: your email address, what Maya knows about you, your conversations with Maya and your password, which nobody can see, not even us.

A transfer to them takes place only on the safeguard that applies to that recipient at the time: an adequacy decision of the European Commission, including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for recipients certified under it, or the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, included in that provider's data processing terms. You can ask for a copy of the applicable safeguard at welcome@altfactor.ro.

## 8. Security

The measures below are those actually built into the service:

- passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes and must be at least 8 characters long;
- the sign-in key kept on your phone is stored on our side only as a hash, and signing out deletes it;
- signing in is blocked after 5 failed attempts for the same email address and IP address, and password reset emails are limited to 3 a minute for the same address;
- photos are saved again as new images, without their hidden location data;
- only members of our team whose role allows it can open the administration panel, and each section of it checks that permission again;
- the connection to the app and to the website is encrypted (HTTPS).

No security measure is absolute. If we learn of a breach that is likely to put you at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay.

## 9. Deleting your data

Deleting your account, from Settings, removes straight away your account, your profile, your photos, your posts, comments, reviews and likes, the travellers you follow, your conversations with Maya, the record of your acceptance and your sign-in keys. The likes and comments counted on other travellers' posts are recounted at the same moment.

You can also delete, one by one, a post, a comment, a review or a conversation with Maya, and clear any field of your profile.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data and receive a copy of it (Art. 15), to correct it (Art. 16), to have it erased (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21).

Most of these you can exercise yourself in the app: edit your profile, delete what you published or delete your account. For everything else, write to welcome@altfactor.ro. We may ask for information that confirms it is you. We answer within one month; for complex requests we may extend that by two more months, and we will tell you why within the first month. Requests are free.

You also have the right to complain to the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru 28-30, Sector 1, 010336 Bucharest, www.dataprotection.ro, or to the supervisory authority of the country where you live or work, and to go to court.

## 11. Automated decisions

We take no decision about you based solely on automated processing. The community shows the newest posts first, or the posts about the places closest to you; places and experiences are listed by category and name. Maya's answers are generated automatically, but they decide nothing about you. Our moderators are people.

## 12. Children

The app is for people aged 16 and over, which is when Romanian law allows a person to consent on their own to the processing of their data in online services. We do not ask for your date of birth. If you believe that a child under 16 has created an account, write to welcome@altfactor.ro and we will delete it.
